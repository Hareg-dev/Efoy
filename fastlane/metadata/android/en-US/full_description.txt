Efoy is a point-of-sale, stock and profit app built for Ethiopian shops. It runs
entirely on your phone: no account, no subscription server, no internet needed.

SELL
Tap products into a cart, change quantities, or override a price with your
password. Settle in full, take a part payment, or record the sale on credit with
the customer's name. Cash, Telebirr, CBE Birr, Amhara Bank or a mix, with change
worked out for you. Hold an order and resume it when the queue moves on.

WHEN A CUSTOMER OWES YOU
A credit or part-paid sale keeps its full value, so your profit stays on the day
the goods left the shop. The unpaid part appears in "Who owes me" with the
customer's name, phone and every instalment taken since. Taking money later only
moves that balance; the original sale is never edited, and nobody is chased for a
sale you have undone.

STOCK THAT ADDS UP
Weighted-average costing on every restock, so margin is real. See stock value,
low-stock and sold-out products on the home screen, and keep a restock log with
what each batch cost.

EXPENSES AND REAL PROFIT
Record rent, salaries, transport, utilities, airtime and supplies by category.
Restocking is treated as capital, not an expense, because it becomes stock.
Profit is split the way a shop actually works: today is selling minus the cost
of the goods sold; the week also subtracts running costs; the month subtracts
salaries too. The profit card prints every deduction and shows the cash you
actually took beside the money still with customers.

REPORTS
A business report with daily trading, your best day, product trends ranked by
profit, operating costs by category and restock history.

A MISTAKE IS FIXABLE
The last three sales on the home screen each have Undo. It puts the units back on
the shelf and clears any balance the sale opened, in one step, and asks first.

YOUR SHOP, YOUR LOOK
Store name, owner name and photo. Light, dark or follow the system, six accent
colours, and English or Amharic. Export an Excel report, take a full JSON backup,
and restore it on a new phone.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
No analytics, no ads, no accounts. Your sales and stock stay in a local database
on the device; nothing is uploaded anywhere. The app works in airplane mode.
